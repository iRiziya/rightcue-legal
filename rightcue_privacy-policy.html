Privacy Policy for RightCue

Last updated: August 24, 2026

RightCue ("the app," "we," "us") is developed by Rinkal Chahodiya. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it's used, and what it does not do.

What RightCue does

RightCue helps you stay aware of your priorities when you spend time in apps you've chosen to be reminded about. It does this using Apple's on-device Screen Time APIs and local on-device notifications.

Information we access & Third-Party Services

App usage detection (Screen Time / Family Controls). With your permission, RightCue uses Apple's DeviceActivity and Family Controls frameworks to detect when you've spent a set amount of time in an app you've chosen. This detection happens entirely on your device. RightCue never receives the names of the apps you've selected, cannot see what you do inside them, and does not transmit this information anywhere — Apple's frameworks are specifically designed so that this data never leaves your device.

Tasks you create. Task titles, due dates, and priority flags you enter are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData storage. Your private task text is never uploaded to any custom servers.

Analytics and Diagnostics (Google Firebase). RightCue uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand app stability, feature usage, and improve app performance. This service collects anonymous technical diagnostics (crash stack traces, non-fatal errors, app launch times) and aggregated app interaction events (such as task completion funnels and feature usage). Task titles are categorized locally on-device for high-level intent categories before logging to preserve your privacy. For more information, please see Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchase information (RevenueCat & Apple). If you subscribe to RightCue Pro, purchase processing is handled by Apple's App Store and by RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. RevenueCat receives standard anonymous transaction data (such as subscription status and renewal dates) needed to verify your subscription. RightCue never collects or has access to your payment card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

House Cross-Promotion. RightCue may display non-targeted promotions for other indie apps developed by the same creator. No personal data or cross-app tracking identifiers are shared with third-party advertising networks.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, login, or email address to use the app
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to data brokers or advertisers
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites (No ATT / IDFA tracking)
  • We do not transmit your raw, private task text to external servers

Data retention and deletion

Task data lives locally on your device and is deleted when you delete the app. You can revoke Screen Time permissions at any time in Settings → Screen Time → RightCue, or disable notifications in Settings → Notifications → RightCue.

Children's privacy

RightCue is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
