Privacy Policy for RightCue

Last updated: July 28, 2026

RightCue ("the app," "we," "us") is developed by Rinkal Chahodiya. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it's used, and what it does not do.

What RightCue does

RightCue helps you stay aware of your priorities when you spend time in apps you've chosen to be reminded about. It does this entirely using Apple's on-device Screen Time APIs — it does not use tracking, analytics, or advertising technology of any kind.

Information we access

App usage detection (Screen Time / Family Controls). With your permission, RightCue uses Apple's DeviceActivity and Family Controls frameworks to detect when you've spent a set amount of time in an app you've chosen. This detection happens entirely on your device. RightCue never receives the names of the apps you've selected, cannot see what you do inside them, and does not transmit this information anywhere — Apple's frameworks are specifically designed so that this data never leaves your device or reaches the app developer.

Tasks you create. Task titles and priority flags you enter are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). This data is not uploaded to any server we operate, and we have no access to it.

Notifications. With your permission, RightCue sends local notifications generated entirely on your device. No notification content is sent through any third-party notification service that could read its contents.

Purchase information. If you subscribe to RightCue Premium, purchase processing is handled by Apple's App Store and by RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. RevenueCat receives standard purchase/transaction data (such as subscription status and purchase dates) needed to verify your subscription — RightCue does not separately collect payment details, and never has access to your card or bank information. See RevenueCat's own privacy policy for details on their data handling: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

What we do not do

  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
  • We do not require an account, login, or email address
  • We do not sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites

Data retention and deletion

Task data lives on your device and is deleted if you delete the app. To stop RightCue from accessing app-usage data, you can revoke Screen Time permission at any time in Settings → Screen Time → RightCue, or disable notifications in Settings → Notifications → RightCue.

Children's privacy

RightCue is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your region).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
