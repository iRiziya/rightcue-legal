Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track more than one distraction app?
Upgrade to RightCue Premium from the Settings tab to track unlimited apps and set unlimited priority tasks. The free plan tracks one of each.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple. Go to Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → RightCue, and you can cancel any time. You can also tap "Restore Purchases" in the app's Settings tab if a purchase isn't reflecting correctly.

Is my data private?
Yes. RightCue never sees which apps you've selected or what you do inside them — all detection happens on your device using Apple's Screen Time technology. See our full Privacy Policy for details.

Does RightCue block apps?
No, never. RightCue only sends a gentle reminder — you always stay in full control of your device.
