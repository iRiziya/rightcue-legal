Frequently Asked Questions

What's free, and what is RightCue Pro?
Everything is free: unlimited tracked apps, tasks, and reminders. The Insights tab is free for your first months; after that, RightCue Pro unlocks it forever. RightCue Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. You'll find it in the app's Settings tab.

My purchase isn't showing.
Open the Settings tab and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought RightCue Pro with.

I had a RightCue subscription. What happens now?
It keeps RightCue Pro active until you cancel it. Subscriptions are managed through Apple: go to Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → RightCue.

Can I add a task by voice?
Yes. Tap the microphone in the task's title field and say it — "call mom tomorrow at 5pm" sets the date too. Your voice is turned into text on your iPhone and never leaves it.

Is my data private?
Yes. RightCue never sees which apps you've selected or what you do inside them — all detection happens on your device using Apple's Screen Time technology. See our full Privacy Policy for details.

Does RightCue block apps?
No, never. RightCue only sends a gentle reminder — you always stay in full control of your device.
